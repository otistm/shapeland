# ADR 0011 — Phase 9 is Ice gold-standard, not open world

Status: Accepted

## Context

DESIGN.md §6 item 9 names "open world: streaming, districts, landmark bands, hub, bestiary, corpse
run, color-return." That is a scaling phase. The vertical-slice ledger (`docs/vertical-slice-plan.md`
§12) already ordered **Ice first** because it is the only ability that changes **movement**. Shipping
districts before the movement grammar is gold would violate "gold standards before scaling."

## Decisions

1. **Phase 9 is Ice.** Ice-up `FLAG_AIR_LAND` freezes a slick patch with the same integer d² cutoff
   as fire (`KILL_RANGE2`). Rolling onto ice slides to the next dry cell — extra translation without
   extra quarter-turns. Leap and pivot do not slide. Fire scorch and ice paint are mutually exclusive
   on a cell.
2. **A slide is not a roll.** The quarter-turn invariant still holds: one quarter-turn per *roll*.
   Slide keeps the orientation earned by the roll that entered the ice. Camera feed is linear xz and
   resting destination height, with no extra spin.
3. **Open world stays deferred.** Streaming, districts, landmark bands, hub topology, bestiary,
   corpse run, color-return, Swap, and Imprint are not this phase.
4. **Optional find, not a stage gate.** An ice glyph in the sealed chamber (`(2, -26)`) stamps Ice
   onto the down face. `STAGE_DONE` remains the two-glyph fire+lightning beat. Loadout advice is
   Fire opposite Ice (axis-exclusion); Ice is never auto-equipped.
5. **Look-ahead exp vs prototype lerp** was decided in ADR 0010 and is unchanged here.

## Consequences

- Gate: Ice movement-grammar proofs, existing socket BFS pins unchanged, full regression green.
- Color is earned (pillar 3): cyan slick decals exist only where ice was painted.
- Snapshot `hashWorld` now mixes `iceHash` and `iceTaken`; the golden idle/roll corpus must be
  regenerated when those fields are added.
